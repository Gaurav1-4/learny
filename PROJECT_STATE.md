## 📜 Saved Raw Homework Transcript Archive (Live)

1. **Exact Verbatim Text Preservation**:
   - Every input you type or speak is permanently recorded under `rawInput` in your account's storage.
   - An **"📥 Saved Homework Transcript"** card appears at the top of every course workspace displaying your exact typed/spoken words (e.g. `"${savedRawText}"`).
2. **1-Click "Rewrite with Gemini AI"**:
   - Takes your exact stored text and feeds it directly into the Gemini LLM.
   - Outputs step-by-step KaTeX LaTeX mathematical derivations, chapter topics, difficulty tags, and calendar schedules without re-typing.
3. **Pending vs. Done Interactive Lifecycle**:
   - Interactive `Done? [Yes / No]` toggles.
   - 1-click `Edit`, `25m Focus Session`, and `SM-2 Flashcard` actions.
