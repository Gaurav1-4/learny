## ⚡ 1-Click "Re-run with AI" & Verbatim Raw Text Archive (Live)

1. **Zero Re-Typing Requirement**:
   - Every raw homework text you type or speak is permanently saved under `rawInput`.
   - The top action bar features a dedicated **`"✨ Re-run with AI"`** button.
   - Clicking it automatically passes all stored raw homework through the Gemini LLM to synthesize fresh KaTeX equations, problem numbers, methods, and schedules without re-speaking or re-typing.
2. **Interactive Course Workspaces**:
   - `Pending` and `Done` tabs.
   - `Done? [Yes / No]` toggles.
   - 1-click `Edit`, `25m Focus Session`, and `SM-2 Flashcard` actions.
